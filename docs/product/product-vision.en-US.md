# Product Vision

## Vision

Game Market Intelligence is a free, source-aware decision-support platform that helps game producers move from an early concept to a better-supported next-step decision.

## Problem

Early market research is fragmented, repetitive, and vulnerable to famous-title bias, inconsistent classifications, unclear provenance, and manual reconciliation.

## Decision workflow

```text
Game idea
    ↓
Discover candidate comparables
    ↓
Refine product relevance
    ↓
Inspect provenance and original sources
    ↓
Evaluate commercial evidence
    ↓
Assess market context
    ↓
Support the next decision
```

## Product direction

GMI should help producers:

- discover famous and less-visible comparable games;
- distinguish broad similarity from meaningful comparability;
- understand platforms, releases, companies, and product traits;
- inspect official, curated, community, and estimated data correctly;
- control the trade-off between confidence and coverage;
- continue research through verified original references;
- later compare sales-first commercial evidence and contextual signals.

## Boundaries

GMI is not a store, review platform, complete database replica, automatic production decision-maker, or guarantee of success.

It organizes and explains evidence while preserving uncertainty and source identity.

## Long-term commercial evidence

Priority:

1. units sold;
2. revenue;
3. estimated owners;
4. downloads;
5. active players;
6. concurrent players;
7. reviews, wishlists, followers, and related signals.

Metrics must preserve platform, region, period, method, source, and whether the value is official or estimated.

## Success direction

Success is not measured only by record count. The product succeeds when a producer completes a useful first research pass with fewer fragmented searches, less manual organization, and clearer evidence.
